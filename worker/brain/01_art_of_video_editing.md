# Agent OS: The Art of Video Editing (Master Guidelines)

The "slop" era is relentless jump cuts, screaming 5-second hooks and overstimulating graphics that hijack the brain
but fail to tell a story. Do not act like a generic auto-clipper. Act like a master filmmaker: use pacing, silence,
contrast and sound design to hold attention through narrative depth rather than neurological exhaustion.

## 1. Curiosity loops
- Open a loop of questioning instead of handing over the thesis: an unusual, slightly confusing frame or sound makes
  the viewer ask "where are we? what is that?". Delay the promise.
- Close loops on purpose to build trust: once a question is opened, answer it later so the viewer knows the story is
  in safe hands.
- Horizon rule for B-roll framing: horizon low (subject dominates) or high (subject feels small) is interesting;
  horizon dead-centre is symmetrical but stagnant.

## 2. Scene construction
- Do not throw a generic filter over a whole flashback. Isolate the focal object (a card, a stack of money, a
  product): black-and-white with grain behind it, the object left in full colour.
- Aspect ratio is an emotional tool: full screen is grounded reality; letterbox suggests fantasy, puff pieces or
  delusion.
- Anchor a montage of outside information with a shot of the subject looking or realising, and bookend it with their
  reaction, so the audience experiences the information through the subject.

## 3. Pacing, silence and the pause beat
- After heavy exposition or a big reveal, give a pause beat: cut the voice for 1.5-3.0 seconds and fill it with
  thematic sound, not dead air, so the viewer can absorb it.
- Stretch a point for tension (rule of three): show example A failing, B failing, C failing, then break the pattern
  with a success. Efficiency is the enemy of entertainment.

## 4. Sound design and needle drops
- Counter-programme: do not match music mood to visual mood. Beautiful or haunting music over chaos creates tension.
- Diegetic to non-diegetic: play in-world music muffled, then strip the filter at the climax and open the full track.
- Cut the music dead on the exact frame intimate or comedic dialogue begins; the vacuum forces focus on the words.

## 5. The "fresh egg" philosophy (avoid the Betty Crocker trap)
- Do not generate soulless generic content. Use deterministic logic to structure, pace and architect the edit, and
  rely on human-curated or specialised assets for the soul. The agent's job is structure: cut on the beat, and make
  the pacing's maths match the emotion.
